Investments buy now, sell when demand arrives

A watchlist of items that should be worth more later in WoW: Forever's lifecycle. The reasons and dates are written by hand in config.toml ([[investment]] and [[catalyst]]); the numbers and the signal are recalculated from the price history on every run. Buy zone = in the cheapest third of the range seen so far, or 5%+ under the 7-day average. Pricey = in the top fifth of the range, or 10%+ over the 7-day average. Sell window = within 21 days after the catalyst, at least 15% over the week before it. The range only covers the days tracked so far, so early signals are rough.
